How it works

Pay upfront. We run. You receive.

No haggling, no guesswork, no “maybe it’s in stock.” You tell us exactly what you want, we go get it.

01You send the exact product.Model number, photo, or link — we confirm availability and final price.
02You pay upfront.M-Pesa, bank transfer, or card. Payment covers the item + our errand fee.
03We run the town.We visit trusted suppliers, verify the item, test it where possible, and negotiate the best price.
04You get photo/video proof.Before we leave the shop — serial number, condition, accessories.
05We deliver to your door.Nairobi same-day, countrywide via courier or our rider network.
06You confirm and enjoy.Warranty documents handed over, and we stay reachable for support.
